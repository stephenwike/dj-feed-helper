# DJ Feed Helper

DJ Feed Helper connects [DJ Feed](https://feed.beyondlinedance.com) to **VirtualDJ** on your computer. VirtualDJ keeps playing your music; DJ Feed follows it. Approved requests go straight into VirtualDJ, and dancers see what VirtualDJ is playing.

## Download

**[Download DJ Feed Helper for Windows](https://github.com/stephenwike/dj-feed-helper/releases/latest/download/DJFeedHelper.exe)** (newest version, about 85 MB)

You don't need to start here: in DJ Feed, open your event's controller and click **Set up VirtualDJ** in the VirtualDJ box. It walks you through everything, one step at a time, including this download. The full guide is at [feed.beyondlinedance.com/help/virtualdj](https://feed.beyondlinedance.com/help/virtualdj).

## Is it safe?

- **It never controls your decks.** DJ Feed Helper never starts, stops or pauses VirtualDJ. It only adds songs to VirtualDJ's sidelist or Automix list, and reads what's playing, through VirtualDJ's own Network Control extension.
- **It only runs while you use DJ Feed.** It has no window and doesn't start with Windows. DJ Feed starts it, and it stops by itself a few minutes after you close DJ Feed.
- **It stays on your computer.** It talks only to VirtualDJ on this computer and to DJ Feed.
- **It installs itself** for your Windows account (no administrator rights), and **you remove it like any app**: Settings → Apps → Installed apps → DJ Feed Helper → Uninstall.

## "Windows protected your PC"

DJ Feed Helper is new and not yet listed with Microsoft, so Windows may warn you the first time you open it. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. A Microsoft Store version, which installs without this warning, is on its way.
